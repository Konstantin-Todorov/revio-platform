A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Do I need all the products?+

No. Start with the Revio product you need and add others later. RevioDirect is the guest-facing booking experience configured through CRS.

What happens when I add another product?+

Revio products share core hotel data. Adding another workspace does not require a new copy of your rooms and reservations.

Can I keep my existing software?+

Tell us which systems you use. We will confirm the supported connection and data flow before proposing a setup. Compatibility depends on the provider.

Can I see it with my hotel's setup?+

Yes. Book a demo and tell us about your property, current tools and the workflow you want to improve.
