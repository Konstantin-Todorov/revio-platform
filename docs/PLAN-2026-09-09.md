# What next — payments, integrations, VAT, and the shape of the console

Claude Code, 2026-09-09. Answers four founder questions and proposes a split with Codex.
Assessment and design. Nothing below is built yet except where it says shipped.

---

## 1 · Stripe — the model changes everything, and it is the good version

The question was *"a setting where we program all the connections and there are fields I add the keys
from Stripe"* — **for our clients**. That is a different product from the one I was answering
before, and it is much better.

**Two possible models, and they are not close:**

| | Who holds the Stripe account | What we are |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stripe Connect** | We do; hotels are sub-accounts | A payment facilitator. Their guests' money flows through our platform account. KYC, payouts, disputes, and a regulatory posture we do not want |
| **The hotel's own keys** ✅ | The hotel does | An integration. Their guest pays them directly. We never touch, hold or route a cent |

**The second is what was asked for and it is the right one.** A hotel that already has Stripe pastes
its own keys; the money never enters our account, so we are not moving somebody else's money and the
whole facilitator question disappears. It is also honest to a hotel: it is *their* gateway, their
payout schedule, their disputes.

⚠️ It does not remove PCI entirely — card data must go straight from the guest's browser to Stripe,
never through our server. Stripe Elements already does this and `@revio/payments` is already built
that way (token and last4 only, no card fields anywhere).

### What the screen has to do

The pattern already exists in this codebase — `ConnectivityCredential` for Channex — and it got three
things right that this must copy:

1. **Encrypted at rest**, shown afterwards as a hint only (`sk_live_••••4242`). A key that can be
   read back out of the UI is a key that leaks in a screenshot.
2. **Test before trust.** A "Test connection" button that makes one real call and reports what came
   back. Channex taught this the hard way: an unauthenticated request returns `401` with no `data`
   key, and `data.length ?? 0` reads it as an empty account. **Check the status code, never the
   shape of the answer.**
3. **Mode is explicit and never inferred.** Stripe keys carry their mode in the prefix (`sk_test_` /
   `sk_live_`) — validate it against a mode the hotel chose, and refuse a mismatch. A live key
   pasted into a sandbox field is money moving when somebody believed it was a rehearsal.

### The one that will actually bite

**A test key and a live key produce different customer, payment-method and intent ids, and they do
not exist in each other's account.** Switch a hotel from sandbox to live and every stored
`stripeCustomerId` and card token becomes a dangling reference — the guarantee on a future booking
silently stops working.

So the token must record which mode created it, and switching modes must say plainly what it
invalidates. This is the same class as the Channex sandbox/production trap already documented in
`CLAUDE.md`, and it has cost a day there once already.

## 2 · Integration centre — now it earns its place

I said "not yet" when there was one integration. With the AI assistant added there are **four**, and
that changes the answer:

| Integration | State today |
| --- | --- |
| **Stripe** (hotel's own) | Not built — §1 |
| **Channex** | Built; per-tenant credential exists but is operator-only |
| **AI assistant** (Claude API / MCP) | Not built |
| Accounting export | Not built |

Build it as **one page with one shape per connection**: name, what it does, connected/not, a test
button, when it was last checked. Adding the fifth should be a row, not a project.

⚠️ **The AI one is not like the others.** A Stripe key moves money; an AI key sends a hotel's data
to a third party. Different question, different consent, and it must be per-hotel opt-in with what
is sent stated plainly. Worth designing at the point we build it, not assuming it is another key
field.

## 3 · VAT — researched, and there is one real defect

Asked to settle it rather than wait for an accountant. Three questions, three answers, evidence
attached.

### What is already right — verified in the code today

| Line | Category | Rate | Correct? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Accommodation | `reduced` | 9% | ✅ Art. 66а ЗДДС — permanent reduced rate for настаняване |
| Breakfast **inside** one announced room price | `accommodation` → `reduced` | 9% | ✅ one announced price ⇒ 9%, fiscal group "Г" |
| Breakfast, minibar, restaurant sold **separately** | `standard` | 20% | ✅ restaurant services returned to 20% on 2025-01-01 |
| Our SaaS invoice to a hotel | — | 20% | ✅ |

`defaultTaxCategory` in `apps/pms/lib/posting.ts` does this correctly and needs no change.

### ⚠️ The defect: tourist tax is inside the VAT base, and we charge 0% on it

НАП's published position is explicit: **туристическият данък се включва в данъчната основа** — the
base for a night is the accommodation charge **plus** the tourist tax, and VAT is charged on the
total. It is shown as its own line on the document, which is presumably why it was modelled as a
separate zero-rated fee.

Our code returns `0` for `city_tax` (`rateFor` in `apps/pms/lib/invoice.ts`), so:

```
€192 room + €3 city tax
  today:    192 @ 9%  +  3 @ 0%     → VAT €15.85
  correct:  195 @ 9%                 → VAT €16.10
```

Understated by roughly €0.25 a booking — small per stay, wrong on every tax document, and it is a
tax document.

**Zero real invoices have been issued to a real client, so fixing it now costs nothing.** After the
first one it is a correction, not a change.

**Fix:** `city_tax` charges at the reduced rate rather than zero, while keeping its own line and its
own label on the invoice. `exempt` and `null` keep returning 0.

### Still genuinely open — and these do need a person

1. **Are we VAT-registered?** From 2026-01-01 the threshold is **EUR 51,130 on a calendar year**, not
   a rolling twelve months. `Company.standardVatPct` defaults to 20 with no registration check
   behind it. Charging VAT unregistered, and failing to register after crossing mid-year, are both
   real and neither is a code question.
2. **Euro changeover** — dual display and invoices spanning the transition.
3. **Fiscalization** — a hotel taking cash has НАП device obligations. `TaxInvoice.fiscalRef` is the
   seam; see `docs/specs/BG-FISCALIZATION-RESEARCH.md`.

## 4 · The console's shape

The tabs were the small half. The real request is that thirteen flat sidebar links are chaotic, and
the reference is right: **a vertical section list, and horizontal tabs inside a section that has
several views of one thing.**

Both patterns already exist here — `SettingsNav` (vertical, four apps) and the client page tabs
(horizontal, shipped today). What is missing is applying the first to the operator's main menu.

**Grouping, using existing routes only — no route changes, no schema:**

| Group | Routes |
| --- | --- |
| **Today** | `/overview` |
| **Customers** | `/clients`, `/leads` |
| **Support** | `/support` |
| **Revenue** | `/plans`, `/billing` |
| **Operations** | `/health`, `/errors`, `/connectivity` |
| **Product** | `/analytics`, `/platform-history` |
| **Security & settings** | `/settings`, `/auth-log` |

⚠️ Two things must survive the tidy-up, both with reasons written in the code:
- **The auth log stays one click away**, not nested inside Settings — *"it is read when something has
  gone wrong, and a screen you have to remember lives inside another one is a screen nobody finds in
  a hurry."*
- **Plans stays above Billing** — the price list is the decision, invoices are the consequence.

### More about the client — what the big consoles actually show

Compared with how mature B2B consoles present an account, we are missing three things, and none
needs a new table:

1. **A trend, not a number.** Every figure on the client page is today's value. "38 rooms, €97/mo,
   4 channels" does not say whether they are growing or dying. A sparkline of their monthly bookings
   over twelve months answers the only question a renewal call cares about, and `PickupSnapshot`
   plus reservations already hold it.
2. **Health as one score with its parts visible.** Stripe, Intercom and every CRM lead with one
   number — usage, support load, payment history, tenure — and let you open it. We have all four
   inputs (`clientAttention`, the new usage analytics, invoices, `createdAt`) and show them in four
   different places.
3. **A timeline that includes what they did**, not only what we wrote and the milestones we derive.
   First booking, first channel connected, went quiet — the shape of the relationship.
