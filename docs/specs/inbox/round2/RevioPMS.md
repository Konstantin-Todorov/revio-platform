## ***5 · THE PART THAT'S HARD TO COPY (the moat — Desire, with proofs)***

***Kicker:****THE PART THAT'S HARD TO COPY*

## ***What RevioPMS does that the alternatives can't.***

*Consequences of how the platform is built — not features a competitor can ship in a sprint.*

***✓ A room out of order stops selling everywhere, by itself.****Flag it at the front desk or in maintenance and it leaves sale on every channel that moment — not because something syncs, but because it's the same availability the channels were reading all along. Proof — verified in production: taking a unit out of order produced a successful channel push with no further action.*

***✓ The bill can't disagree with the reservation.****The folio is built from the rate the booking was made on, so accounting and reporting reconcile by construction. There's no month-end where two systems have to be argued into agreement. Proof — a direct booking quoted at €195 all-in produces a folio of €192 room + €3 city tax. The same number, from the same source.*

***✓ Housekeeping runs from a phone — each person sees only their part.****Dirty → in progress → awaiting inspection → ready, updated in the corridor. A cleaner sees their rooms; a supervisor sees the floor. Nobody's handed the whole system to learn. Proof — role-scoped views, not a permissions checkbox on the same crowded screen everyone else uses.*

***✓ It runs on top of what you already have.****You don't have to replace a working reservation system to fix operations. Take the operations layer, keep the rest, and add the others when it suits you. Proof — each product is sold and used on its own; the shared core is how they cooperate, not a requirement to buy them together.*

***✓ Nothing sensitive to lose.****Payments are recorded as a method and an amount. Where a card is genuinely taken, it goes straight to the payment provider — we hold a token and four digits. Proof — there's no card number stored anywhere in the platform.*
