## ***5 · THE PART THAT'S HARD TO COPY (the moat — Desire, with proofs)***

***Kicker:****THE PART THAT'S HARD TO COPY*

## ***What RevioCRS does that the alternatives can't.***

*Consequences of how the platform is built — not features a competitor can ship in a sprint.*

***✓ The numbers are computed, not maintained.****Occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, pickup and lead time come from the reservation record itself. There's no spreadsheet to keep current — and therefore no month where somebody forgot. Proof — one formula drives the dashboard, the reports and the CSV export, so the three can never disagree.*

***✓ The room is held before the form is saved.****The moment your agent picks a room, the inventory is locked. On the phone with a guest reading out a card number, that's the difference between a sale and an apology. Proof — holds subtract from the same availability every channel reads, and they expire on their own if the call goes nowhere.*

***✓ A change is safe before it's committed.****Move a stay to new dates and the new dates are checked first. The old room is only given up once the new one is certain — you're never left exposed between the two. Proof — it avoids the classic failure: a change that frees a room, fails, and leaves the guest with nothing.*

***✓ It tells you what commission actually cost — and admits what it can't know.****Commission you paid is a fact, shown per channel. Commission you avoided is an estimate, and left blank when there's nothing to base it on — rather than filled with a flattering guess. Proof — the cost-of-distribution screen returns no number instead of an invented one. It's the same function that prices the direct-booking argument.*

***✓ Your channel work stays in one place.****Per-OTA mapping and pushing belong in a channel manager, not scattered through your reservation system. Use RevioLink, or keep the channel manager you already have. Proof — one connected channel manager, one place to look when a channel misbehaves.*
